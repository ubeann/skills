# AI-default motifs

What an image model draws when a prompt leaves a decision open. Each one marks art as generated. Grow this list: when the owner calls something "AI slop" and a motif explains it, add a row with where it was seen.

| Motif | What it looks like | Replace with | Seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floating island | a patch of ground with a dirt or rock cliff hanging under it, in the sky | a ground plane that runs to the frame edge, or no ground and a flat card colour | `art/cast` round 5, every card |
| Hatching on flat art | thin scratchy lines on dirt, rock or shadow under a flat-shaded character | two flat tones per surface | `art/cast` round 5 |
| Unpaletted earth | olive greens, browns and ochres that no palette asked for, filling the world | the bible's world colours, named in hex in the prompt | `art/cast` round 5 |
| Stray holding | a hand gripping a rod, bar or handle that belongs to nothing | the object's own handle, named in the prompt | `art/cast` banner, round 5 |
| Smudge marks | a blot of a costume or skin colour beside a mouth or eye | a clean face from the face kit | `art/cast` banner, round 5 |
| Light rays behind the subject | a radial sunburst or god rays from behind a centred figure | the bible's one light, from its side | common |
| Dust and sparkle | floating particles, bokeh dots, sparkles with no source | nothing, or the bible's emanata where an emotion needs one | common |
| Glossy everything | a wet highlight on every surface, plastic sheen on flat art | the bible's rendering on every surface | common |
| Even rim light | a glowing outline on every object regardless of the light | one rim on the side away from the light, on the lead only | common |
| Symmetric centre | the subject dead centre, symmetric props on both sides, horizon at half height | the lead on a third, a shape that carries the eye | common |
| Swirl clouds | clouds as spirals or cotton balls with gradient shading | flat cloud shapes with the cast's outline, or a plain sky | common |
| Text squiggles | letter-like marks on signs, books, screens and labels | blank shapes; real text goes on in code | common |
| Merged props | two objects fused, an extra limb or finger, a prop passing through another | one change per call, with the prop named and placed | common |
| Stock metaphor | lightbulb for an idea, rocket for launch, magnifier over a cylinder for data, compass for direction | the owned metaphor from the bible's subject vocabulary | the handbook set |
| Over-detail at small size | fine detail everywhere that turns to noise at the display size | detail on the one identifying piece, plain shapes elsewhere | common |
