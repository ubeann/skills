# Composing banners, cards and scenes

A layout built from approved figures fails in ways the identity check cannot see: nine figures of one size on one baseline with no lead, a costume that melts into a card of its own colour, cutouts floating on a flat ground. This repo's first cast banner and cards did all three, after four review rounds on the character and none on the layout. A layout gets its own round.

## Layout round

1. **One composition reference per layout.** Measure it as `moodboard` measures any reference: where the lead figure sits and its share of the frame, how many depth layers, where the light comes from, where the text sits. Everything else from it is ignored.
2. **Two or three layouts per target, in greyscale.** Blocks or the figures' silhouettes on the target's real canvas, no colour, each with its value pattern named (below). Show them as one gallery.
3. **Write the pick down.** In `art/<name>/README.md`: the layout, its value pattern, the ground colour, and the pose each figure takes in it. A decision left in chat is lost by the build.
4. **Make the poses the layout needs.** A card pose is rarely a banner pose. A figure whose pose does not exist yet goes back through step 5 of `SKILL.md` with that pose as the one change.

Done when the owner picked one layout per target and every figure it places has an approved pose in it.

## Rules

### Hierarchy

- **One lead.** One figure at 1.3 to 2x the others, chosen by contrast of action or expression (the one awake among sleepers), often cropped by the bottom edge. The others group in two or three clusters at middle distance, each busy with its job ([video at 5:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nry7K2y4ypc&t=345s); [post](https://x.com/tyka_dominik/status/2117921316242096490)).
- **A shape carries the eye**: the lead at the apex of a triangle, or the cast on an L or a circle; small accents mark the next stop ([video at 2:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f5BK66Bjup0&t=165s)).
- **Interaction over lineup.** Figures that look at, hand to or lean on each other read as a cast; a row of front poses reads as a catalogue ([video at 12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FPs0UXxy1uM&t=756s)).
- The rejected default: title on top, subject in the middle, flat ground, no light. "A slide that wiggles" ([video at 3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zk4Qzd-NJ_0&t=214s)).

### Depth and grounding

- **Three layers**, one to three elements each; the sky does not count ([video at 6:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSyVCx8la_w&t=369s)).
- **A ground plane first**: horizon and an average-height mark, figures sized from their feet ([video at 4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rm0wi0afoKM&t=293s)). A contact shadow under every figure ([video at 1:01:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rm0wi0afoKM&t=3712s)).
- **A foreground layer cuts the bodies**: clouds, a desk edge, a crate, blurred, across the bottom ([video at 7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSyVCx8la_w&t=436s)).
- **Relight cutouts for the scene**: one rim light on the same side of every figure, a ground tint on top. Relighting and a contact shadow ground a cutout; a white sticker outline only isolates it, so keep the sticker for icon tiles.
- **A scene can be generated whole and split.** One master image, then background, figures and a near-camera corner cut into layers: "a space, not a slide" ([video at 4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zk4Qzd-NJ_0&t=282s)). Collage from separate cutouts when every figure must stay exact.

### Value and colour

- **Value pattern first**, then hue ([video at 1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BuJY169z_F4&t=61s)). Name one: dark lead on a light ground with grey corners, light lead on a dark ground, or a dark band under light faces.
- **The ground is never a tint of the costume.** Use the costume accent's complement, a neutral, or a value at least 30 L* away ([video at 4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f5BK66Bjup0&t=245s)). A dark-bodied cast on a mid blue separates by its outline alone.
- **A saturated full-bleed ground fights saturated figures.** One artist tried yellow, then cream, then near-black, and kept the dark ground with the colour moved onto the figures' rim light ([video at 6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m4TFHn6vkrI&t=376s)).
- **Focus by chroma and detail**: full colour and highlights on the lead; secondary figures darker, lower in chroma, or slightly blurred ([video at 5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BuJY169z_F4&t=343s); [video at 1:52:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rm0wi0afoKM&t=6751s)).
- **Contrast falls off toward the bottom edge**; a hard band of a second colour there pulls the eye down ([video at 2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BuJY169z_F4&t=171s)).
- **Shade with a darker, more saturated local colour**, never black at low opacity ([video at 6:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m4TFHn6vkrI&t=407s)).

### Cards

- **The figure overlaps its frame**: the backing shape is narrower than the figure, which breaks its edges and bleeds off the bottom; a head can run to 110% of the panel width ([video at 1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSyVCx8la_w&t=91s); [post](https://x.com/heysatya_/status/2118381016780067043)).
- **A backing shape per subject** (a film frame, a slide, a compass rose), not one panel repeated ([video at 1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSyVCx8la_w&t=74s)).
- **The figure uses its prop**, not stands beside it ([video at 1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSyVCx8la_w&t=108s)).
- **One outline.** The art's ink line, a sticker rim and a panel border stacked around one figure read as clip art; keep one.
- **Content that sells the character**: a personality chip (PROUD, CURIOUS), one line in the character's voice, a title that is the costume's joke, a collectible mark ("No. 06") ([post](https://x.com/heysatya_/status/2117169115886707100); [post](https://x.com/adrianabelarde/status/2117896840633098515)). A face inset beside the full figure helps where the full figure is small ([video at 27:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rm0wi0afoKM&t=1646s)).
- **A set reads as one strip** when props cross from one card into the next ([post](https://x.com/heysatya_/status/2118381016780067043)).
- Without a scene, a plain muted backdrop with the full figure and a fixed mark position beats half a scene ([video at 16:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nry7K2y4ypc&t=972s)).

### Text

- Text sits in a calm safe zone and is never generated; the type and its scale come from `DESIGN.md` when one exists.
- The character can enter the type: eyes in a letter's counter, a prop replacing a letter ([post](https://x.com/adrianabelarde/status/2117896840633098515)).

## Build and check

Compose in code (HTML or SVG rendered by headless Chrome, or Pillow) from the cleaned figures, so text stays sharp and every figure stays exact. Render each layout twice: with its figures, and without them as a **plate** of the same size and file name in a second folder. Then:

```sh
python3 <skill-dir>/references/compose_check.py art/<name>/out/layouts/*.png --plates art/<name>/out/plates/ --sheet art/<name>/compose.png
```

`camo` is the share of coloured figure pixels that share a hue and a value with the ground around them. On this repo's first cards it measured 21 to 53% on seven of nine; the same cards on a cream ground with a complementary panel measured 0 to 6%.

Then open `compose.png`, one row per layout: colour, greyscale, greyscale blurred, and the camouflaged pixels in magenta. In the blurred column one shape leads and the faces stay readable. Then look at each layout at its real size in its real place: a README card at about 260 px in GitHub's light and dark themes, a social preview at 600 px wide ([video at 13:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nry7K2y4ypc&t=821s)).

Done when `compose_check.py` passes every layout, the blurred column shows one lead per layout, and each layout has been seen at its real size on every ground it ships on.
